# Chapter 00: What Sticks?

## Core Idea
A sticky idea is understandable, memorable, and effective in changing behavior. The SUCCESs framework (Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories) provides six principles to overcome the primary villain: the Curse of Knowledge.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The SUCCESs Framework**: A six-principle checklist for making ideas stick — Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories
- **The Curse of Knowledge**: Once you learn something, it's nearly impossible to imagine not knowing it. Experts "tap a tune" that novices hear as incomprehensible Morse code.
- **Tappers and Listeners Experiment**: Demonstrates the Curse of Knowledge — tappers predict 50% guess rate, actual rate is 2.5%

## Key Concepts
- **Sticky idea**: An idea that is understood, remembered, and changes behavior
- **Curse of Knowledge**: The cognitive bias that makes experts unable to think like novices
- **SUCCESs**: The six-principle checklist (not "SUCCESS" — one S is missing intentionally)

## Key Takeaways
1. Stickiness is a learnable skill, not an innate gift
2. The Curse of Knowledge is the #1 enemy of effective communication
3. Urban legends are stickier than most corporate messages — study why
4. Any idea can be engineered to stick using the SUCCESs template
