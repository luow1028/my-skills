# Chapter 05: Emotional

## Core Idea
Make people care by appealing to self-interest, identity, or empathy for individuals — not groups or statistics.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Mother Teresa Principle**: "If I look at the mass, I will never act. If I look at the one, I will." People empathize with individuals, not groups.
- **Semantic Stretch**: When powerful words (like "innovative" or "premium") are overused, they lose meaning.
- **WIIFY (What's In It For You)**: Appeal to self-interest, but frame it as the audience's benefit, not yours.
- **Maslow's Basement**: Don't assume others are only motivated by base needs (money, security) while you're driven by higher ideals.
- **Identity Model of Decision-Making**: People choose based on "Who am I? What kind of situation is this? What does someone like me do?"

## Key Concepts
- **Analytical vs. Emotional Hat**: Statistics activate cold analysis; individual stories activate empathy
- **Appealing to self-interest**: Not selfish — it's understanding what the audience values
- **Identity over rationality**: Group identity often trumps rational self-interest in decision-making

## Key Takeaways
1. One person's story > statistics about millions
2. Don't just appeal to self-interest — appeal to identity
3. The Identity Model explains why people vote against their economic interests
4. Semantic stretch erodes your power words — use fresh, specific language
