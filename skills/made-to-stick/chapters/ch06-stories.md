# Chapter 06: Stories

## Core Idea
Stories are mental flight simulators — they don't just convey information, they rehearse the audience for action. Use three core story plots.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Challenge Plot**: Protagonist overcomes formidable odds → inspires perseverance and grit
- **Connection Plot**: People bridge a social/racial/demographic gap → inspires tolerance and love
- **Creativity Plot**: Someone makes a mental breakthrough → inspires innovation and resourcefulness
- **Mental Flight Simulators**: Stories activate the brain's physical and emotional modules, rehearsing us for future situations

## Key Concepts
- **Story as rehearsal**: Stories aren't passive entertainment — they prepare you to act
- **Three plots**: Almost all effective stories fit one of the three core plots
- **Stories over morals**: You can reconstruct a moral from a story, but not a story from a moral

## Key Takeaways
1. Stories are the stickiest form of communication because they simulate experience
2. You don't need to invent stories — spot them in real life
3. Challenge, Connection, Creativity — almost every good story fits one of these
4. A good story IS the strategy, not decoration for the strategy
